## Full description
**Try it on. See your style.**

StyleMirror AI lets you preview clothes and glasses on yourself before you buy.

**👕 AI clothes try-on**
• Upload your photo and a product image of a shirt, trousers or dress
• An AI try-on model generates a preview of you wearing it
• Live progress, retry and download, with an optional private history

**🕶️ Glasses: photo and live**
• Five frame styles: Classic Rectangle, Round Wire, Aviator, Cat-Eye and Wayfarer
• Face landmarks align the frames to your eyes and follow head tilt
• Adjust size, height, tilt and lens tint
• Runs entirely on your device; selfies and camera video are never uploaded

**🧍 3D fashion studio**
• Orbit, zoom and pan a 3D avatar
• Load your own GLB/GLTF avatar and garment models, with file validation
• A clear static-preview label: no fake "cloth simulation" claims

**🔒 Privacy first**
• Explicit consent before any photo goes to the AI provider
• Metadata such as GPS location is stripped from uploads
• Input photos are never stored; saved results are private and auto-deleted
• Delete one result or all of your data anytime
